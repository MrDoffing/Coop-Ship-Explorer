Adding some difficulty could be good but mining is done so often so i think it would be too demanding. Making it simple but satisfying is the goal I think.
Possibly broken chunks should stay floating in cave. Then use a tractor beam to suck chunks in. Not difficult but adds more steps to mining process.

Engine module, shield module could be replaced with better ones but require more energy. Would need energy cells or something to aquire more energy points. Or i have a general system is always the same for each ship and give buffs to any of the systems. But unsure if it would be used. I think weapons would always be prioritized.


## What shall player feel like they are?
The game is sci-fi but managing equipement and tools should feel industrial, dirty and heavy. 
The feeling is more towards Alien (Ridley Scott), Terminator (James Cameron) and second world war.
The player is always in first person. Third person ruin the immersion.
Information should preferably always be present in the world, shown in the equipment or in-world displays.
Decorating the ship with bolt, panels, general grime, flashing lights and computer with green text is the visual direction.
For simplicity, the players wont have any hands. If players are using a held tool to interact, then the tool has all the animations.
### Engineers when repairing ship. Fixing curcuits and enforcing plating.
When the ship is damaged, the engineers are responsible for keeping it intact. While damaged, metal panels at the walls, floors and roof will begin to bend and almost tear off. Coords and circutrly will be sticking out and sparks will flicker. Engineers need to hammer with their wrench to step by step flatten the metal panel and get it into working order. This ensures the hull is strengthened. Gun pods and engines in the mothership can be damaged and break down. For the gun pods, the engineers needs to repair this damage by entering their circutry and rewiring. There is a panel opening at the side that can be interacted with to open and there, there are 4 colored input cords that need to be connected with 4 other output coords. They are color coded and need to be connected with their matching color. To repair the engine, the engineers need to retune the engine by balancing the power load between the hydraulics, cooling and dispensers systems. He does this by moveing the sliders for each system until each system reach their desired threshold. Each slider will impact the other system in different ways so each slider need to be at a specific spot for all the systems to be balanced. Each system is represented with a bar and a line, somewhere on the bar that is its desired threshold.
### Soldiers when manning the turrets.
Player should enter a turret pod. Pod will have a seat in middle and control panel in front. Will be a rounded glass panel with metal strip enforcings. Gun barrel should be visible at each side. Physical aiming reticle should placed infront of window panel, aligned with players vision. Ammo and heat is displayed at top of the control panel. Ammo needs to be loaded outside of the gun pod. Ammo boxes needs to be picked up and installed into ammo slot so containing bullet belt can be fed into gun magazine. The engineers are responsible for reloading the gun when the gunner is manning it. Heat disipates after time but firing when the gun is too hot will damage the weapon system which will need repairs from the engineers in that case.
### Pilots when piloting the mothership.
This isnt star wars with dog-figting. This is slow and steady mining operation in tight space. The pilot needs to be aware of his surroundings, tunnel walls and enemies so he can navigate accordingly. The cockpit has a seat, control panel and a wide window for viewing ahead. The control panel has gauges for fuel, hull integrity and shield status. There is also screens showing camera feed from rear and side cameras. The rear camera is placed at top rear of the ship and looking away from the ship. The side camera are placed at front corner at each side and viewing along the edge of the ship, from the front to back, similarly as a cars side mirrors. There is also cameras at the top and bottom, viewing the belly and spine of ship. These camera are both used for ensuring the ship isnt scraping against the cave but also to find and any aliens scratching on the exterior.
The control panel will have a thrust lever for mananging the engines speed, a gear stick for rotating and acending/descending the ship.
