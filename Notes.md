# Thoughts

Mining is really boring. I think that looking at something and simply pressing a button is never good enough. Other games tend to make the mineral more dynamic so that players need to observe and destroy chunks as the break of. Its bit more complicated but more satisfying. Adding some difficulty could be good but mining is done so often so i think it would be too demanding. Making it simple but satisfying is the goal I think.
Possibly broken chunks should stay floating in cave. Then use a tractor beam to suck chunks in. Not difficult but adds more steps to mining process.
Minerals are everywhere. Maybe clump them together so mining only is done at specific occasions. Mining becomes an event that is taking place. Forcing the ship to pause and hold its position. Maybe hostility should increase only when mining? Its a risk and rewards system. Crucial for buying upgrades but risky encounters.
!TASK! Clump minerals together and make it a time consuming event. Hostility increases only when mining. Risk - rewards system. Mining everything will make hostility reach high. High hostility should be difficult. However then it will be easier to snipe nests...
Would be fun to have a grappling arm that you fire and attach to loose chunks and reel in. Like fishing?
Chunks hide in rock that you need to dig with the mining laser. When the chunks get loose you can grab them. Rock is destroyed where you dig with laser so need to move laser around to dig it out.


Cave system: As of right now the cave system is linear. Maybe add splitting section that feature different encounters (outposts, nests, shops). Have a holographic map at center of ship that show oncoming tunnels and their encounters.

Could add energy system that can be distributed manually between shield, weapons, engine. Is a classic system to have in these games. Then i could have module slots that take up X amount of energy. Engine module, shield module could be replaced with better ones but require more energy. Would need energy cells or something to aquire more energy points. Or i have a general system is always the same for each ship and give buffs to any of the systems. But unsure if it would be used. I think weapons would always be prioritized.

No penalty for dying. Easy to dont care about health when in outpost as you instantly respawn. What should a penalty be? Losing credits? Held crew weapon? Maybe drop your stop but dont loose anything else.

New Cave Enemy: Barnacle
Burrowed in the cave wall and waits for mothership.
When close, it jabs its tongue at the hull, sticking to its panels and slowly pushes the ship towards it. The ship is stuck and cant move until either the barnacles tongue has been destroyed or the entire barnacle.
The ship takes damage when jabbed with tongue but when stuck it only pulls the ship.
Ignores scout ship unless provoked. If provoked it jabbs at scout, pulling it in slowly and destroys it (eating it). Scout cant do anything while jabbed.
