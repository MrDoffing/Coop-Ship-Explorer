# Game Spec
4 Player Coop game. All players manage a spaceship. The ship has a different station the players can use, the cockpit (steering), 2 guns on each opposite side on the ship, and the mining laser to mine minerals on the cave walls. The ship can be damaged and will need repairs which the player will fix. Mission is to travel inside large cave systems in asteroids and mine minerals whilst being under attack by aliens. The aliens will spit, scratch and infiltrate the ship which the player needs to handle by repairing and killing. The mothership has one small scout ship that can scout ahead in the cave but also provide with additional firepower. The scout may need to deal with small critters that are scratching the mothership by using its own weaponry.

Mining minerals will be rewarded as currency when the mission is done which can be used to upgrade the tools and ships the players operate (Mostly the mothership). Each player will have a blaster weapon to deal with aliens that infiltrated the ship. They will also have a tool kit that they can use on damaged machine parts. 

The cave will be large and linear for the mothership to fly through. There will be smaller gaps in the cave that only the scout pod can investigate. On the cave walls there will be minerals sticking out. The minerals will break into smaller chunks when mined. The cave will inhibit free roaming aliens and alien nests that the team needs to kill and destroy. Nests will have egg sacs on the walls that need to be destroyed. Nests spawn enemies indefinitely but spawn more aggressively if agitated. 

There are different minerals in the cave. There are mission important minerals and minerals that may be used to refuel the ship. The fuel minerals will exist in the small gaps of the cave that the small ship need to collect. The mothership can run out of fuel so the scout need to occasionally be used to refuel. A mothership without fuel is more vulnerable to enemy attacks. The scout ship doesnt use fuel to operate.

The mothership has systems that can shutdown if damaged. Mothership is destroyed if the hull integrity reaches zero. Mothership has an energy shield that absorbs damage but which will break at some limit. When the shield is down, the hull takes the damage, the shield will regenerate after some time. When the hull takes damage, the engine and weapons system will take partial damage as well. When the engine or weapons systems are broken, they will not be operable. Players may repair damages before the system breaks. The hull integrity cannot be repaired by players as they are exterior damages the players can't reach.

When the mothership is destroyed the players are returned back to homebase. At homebase the players can buy new ship weapons and stat upgrades. With the improved spaceship the player will try to go deeper than the previous attempt.
