# Backlogg

## What shall players feel like they are?
The game is sci-fi but managing equipement and tools should feel industrial, dirty and heavy. 
The feeling is more towards Alien (Ridley Scott), Terminator (James Cameron) and second world war.
The player is always in first person. Third person ruin the immersion.
Information should preferably always be present in the world, shown in the equipment or in-world displays.
Decorating the ship with bolt, panels, general grime, flashing lights and computer with green text is the visual direction.
For simplicity, the players wont have any hands. If players are using a held tool to interact, then the tool has all the animations.
Sounds should feel heavy and mechanical but never screetch in your ear so keep the volume and pitch practical. Sound should, if applicable, change over time to reflect heat levels, magazine or other capacity based features.
### Engineers when repairing ship. Fixing curcuits and enforcing plating.
When the ship is damaged, the engineers are responsible for keeping it intact. While damaged, metal panels at the walls, floors and roof will begin to bend and almost tear off. Coords and circutry will be sticking out and sparks will flicker. Engineers need to hammer with their wrench to step by step flatten the metal panel and get it into working order. This ensures the hull is strengthened, however this takes some time to do and best done during checkpoints/safe-havens. Gun pods and engines in the mothership can be damaged and break down. For the gun pods, the engineers needs to repair this damage by entering their circutry and rewiring. There is a panel opening at the side that can be interacted with to open and there, there are 4 colored input cords that need to be connected with 4 other output coords. They are color coded and need to be connected with their matching color. To repair the engine, the engineers need to retune the engine by balancing the power load between the hydraulics, cooling and dispensers systems. He does this by moving the sliders for each system until each system reach their desired threshold. Each slider will impact the other system in different ways so each slider need to be at a specific spot for all the systems to be balanced. Each system is represented with a bar and a line somewhere on the bar that is its desired threshold.
### Gunners when manning the turrets.
Player should enter a turret pod. Pod will have a seat in middle and control panel in front. Will be a rounded glass panel with metal strip enforcings. Gun barrel should be visible at each side. Physical aiming reticle should placed infront of window panel, aligned with players vision. Ammo and heat is displayed at top of the control panel. Ammo needs to be loaded outside of the gun pod. Ammo boxes needs to be picked up and installed into ammo slot so containing bullet belt can be fed into gun magazine. The engineers are responsible for reloading the gun when the gunner is manning it. Heat disipates after time but firing when the gun is too hot will damage the weapon system which will need repairs from the engineers in that case.
### Pilots when piloting the mothership.
This isnt star wars with dog-figting. This is slow and steady mining operation in tight caves. The pilot needs to be aware of his surroundings, tunnel walls and enemies so he can navigate accordingly. The cockpit has a seat, control panel and a wide window for viewing ahead. The cockpit is the front most part of the ship so the pilot has a wide view. The control panel has gauges for fuel, hull integrity and shield status. There is also screens showing camera feed from rear, top and bottom and side cameras. The rear camera is placed at top rear of the ship and looking away from the ship (to see any enemy behind). The side cameras are placed at front corner at each side and viewing along the edge of the ship, from the front to back, similarly as a cars side mirrors. The top and bottom cameras is placed so they are viewing the belly and spine of ship. These camera are both used for ensuring the ship isnt scraping against the cave but also to find and any aliens scratching on the exterior.
The control panel will have a thrust lever for managing the engines speed, a gear stick for rotating and acending/descending the ship and lastly the big red button for when things go bad. 

### Fighter Pilots when flying in the scout.
The scout should feel like your in a fighter aircraft. Its tight and there are many intruments close at hand. The seat is integrated into the cockpit and there is a control panel to your left and right. The glass wind shield is wide and high to optimize viewing. 
The ship has gauges or instruments for its heat, cannon ammunition, missile status (shown as 2D ship with missiles on wings, green for available and red for empty), cargo status, thruster speed (colored bar filling up. Should have space for boosting as well) and hull integrity.
The scout has 2 integrated cannons, one at each side below the belly. Firing cannons increases heat. They are automatically reloaded when empty. The ship also has 2 heat-seeking missiles (one missile on left and right wings). Enemies can be targeted using right-click and missile may then be fired with left click. Each click fires one. The missiles are reloaded when docking into the mothership. Targeting an enemy shows as an animated crosshair on the cockpit glass that goes from large to small until locked on. The cannon are aimed with an aiming reticle between the pilot and glass window.
Cracks on the window shield appears when the hull is taking heavy damage. When entering critical health the ship warns the pilot and red lights start to blink or pulsate.
The ship has a booster that can be activated using shift key. It drastically increases speed straight ahead but increases heat on the ship. The player should feel the speed when boosting by changing FOV and shaking the ship. The ships heat dissipates after time.

### Space soldiers when walking/firing with their held weapons. 
Players always see the edge of their helmets and there is a few intruments at the bottom of the helmet. For example, the health of the player is displayed at the left bottom of the helmet. The helmets visor auto closes and opens when leaving ship and has some grime on it similar as the motherships glass grime.
The visor cracks when reaching critical health.
The gun is held relaxed when walking and when not firing for a moment. The gun can be hip fired by simply left-clicking but the spread will be big. By right-clicking the player will aim with the gun using its integrated gunsight (different weapons have varying sights). While aiming, the player move slower and fire more accurately. The first shoot is very accurate but continuous firing increases spread. How much ammo the gun has is shown on a display integrated in the gun. Firing the gun eject casings (physics based). Reloading is done by switching magazines. Trying to fire an empty magazine creates a hollow click sound.
Weapon will kick back when firing and create a muzzle flash for each bullet.
Players will start with pistols and later get rifles, shotguns but also energy based weapons.
Energy based weapons will use a heat system which might need to cooldown or otherwise overheat (instead of ammo).
A energy based weapon is for example a lightning thrower, it charges up and emits a lightning bolt when released at closest enemy and chain to any nearby enemies.
The player can bring out a echolocation scanner on their suit (holstering their weapon) which pings like a boat radar, showing a rough estimate of walls, enemies and fellow collegues. The scanner only shows info a certain distance from the player and gets increasingly noisy the further away it is. This radar is only viable when outside ship and inside stations. The player cant use their gun when looking at scanner. The player needs to hold 'Q' to view scanner.
### Abandoned Outpost improvement
Lets make the corridors and rooms more interesting. Corridors shouldn't always be straight, can change direction or even splitt. Rooms should not always be square but rather have extensions or L shaped with furniture or machinery in the middle. Some room might be science labs with liquid filled test tubes with morphed subjects, tables with science equipment. Room might be storage facilities (crates, shelfes) or sleeping quarters (beds, side tables), dining hall (benches and tables). A room could be a security room with displays showing camera feeds on some of the facilities rooms. Some outposts might have a generator (gas fueled) room which they can turn on to turn on some of the working lights and unlock some unpowered doors (possible letting out roaming enemies out as well). There can be an armory room, often spawning crew weapons.
The visual design:
"A retro-futuristic industrial military spaceship corridor, heavy olive-drab and bronze metal construction, thick angular bulkheads, exposed hydraulic pipes, ribbed wall panels, reinforced mechanical door frames, grated steel flooring, symmetrical modular architecture, repeating octagonal structures receding into the distance, warm amber utility lights, subtle grime and wear, deep shadows, low-key cinematic lighting, tactile hard-surface details, late-1990s science-fiction video game atmosphere, grounded and functional design, no sleek minimalist surfaces, no bright neon colors."
Some doors will be locked. To get past it, a player can hack its control panel by entering its panel and interpreting the code. Its a small mini game with 6 tangled coords going from the left to the right. On the left side there are single digit numbers 0-9 and right side have unique colors. On the doors keypad there is 4 colored lights in a horizontal sequence. The player need to map these colors to their number, following the coord and input the 4 digit code in the keypad. If the player guesses wrong 3 times, the door goes into heavy lockdown. Heavy steel shutter slams down in front of door and the corridor starts to flash red. The door cannot be opened. Behind these locked door there is more likely to be upgrades/equipment and enemies.
Enemies: (Dev note. The existing enemies in outposts can be removed or refactored)
One of the enemies should act like the alien from Aliens(Ridley Scott). It isnt as strong but it is bigger than a player and roams the map. When detecting an enemyit will rush, claw at the player a few times and run away to hide. It will come back however. If it gets to low health it will flee, find a place to recuperate and heal.
Barnacles can spawn in the ceiling, these can grab nearby a player so they cant move and damage them slowly. Barnacles dont move and shooting them eliminates them.
There are small critters that move on the wall and ceiling. Almost like face-huggers from Aliens. They jump at the player, damaging them and landing on the floor again. They move back up on the wall or ceiling to attack again. They arent very fast but they can come in numbers. They dont tend to hunt the players but rather stay in their room or corridor. However if they are shot, they will hunt the player within a certain radius of its nest. Their nests are sponge looking extrusions with holes.
There are four legged spider looking aliens that are aggresive and spit at players. They often dont stand still and scurry around between their shots (stands still when spitting). The can walk on walls and ceilings. (Dev note, their legs are procedurally animated to stand on surfaces and take steps when moving too far away from the body)
